# route seconds dump_every required_frames [warm=N or warm-from=ROUTE] [probe settings]
# Required frames are the frames a route must reach, and docs/dev/status.md lists the checkpoints it plays
# to. Seconds are wall-clock with --fast.
#
# warm=N has tools/warm_shaders.py play the route to frame N instead of 900 frames past its last
# required frame. N is a whole number past that frame, and a route has one at most.
#
# warm-from=ROUTE gives the route no warm-up pass of its own, because ROUTE's pass plays what the
# route does. ROUTE has a pass of its own, and every program the route's own pass built has to
# come from one pass or another.
#
# The three -courses routes race one course after another, each for as long as its own route's
# pass played it. A race that follows another starts from another state of the game, and there
# Sky Sands, Magma Flows, Frozen Hillside and Checker Knights each missed programs their own
# passes built late. So Sky Sands and Magma Flows open the two air-ride-courses routes as their
# own routes race them, to frames 14,400 and 12,700, while the other two keep passes of their own.
#
# air-ride-race, air-ride-celestial-valley and air-ride-beanstalk-park get the programs they first
# draw past their own stretches from Sky Sands, which draws them by frame 14,186. Of the keys
# air-ride-race's own pass built, 8 come from passes other than air-ride-courses-1.
#
# Only shader_cache.py diff shows the passes still build every key, so any change to a pass or the
# route it plays means diffing the new warm-up's cache against the last one both ways. A change to
# what the -courses routes race also means running the eight air-ride routes unpaced to frame
# 16,000 again, as under "Chained course passes" in docs/dev/status.md.
