# Quality

How well each feature in [status.md](status.md)'s table is checked today, going by the files that check it. Each one is marked as one of these:

- **Dolphin**: Dragoon is compared with Dolphin at matched game frames, and each checkpoint in the triage file is counted by its class. `within` is under the budget with the state matching, `timing` the same pictures in another order or a few frames apart, `unclear` a difference whose cause isn't known and `bug` a Dragoon bug.
- **Goldens**: our own frame, state or audio hashes cover it. No Dolphin compare does, and a golden only says the output hasn't changed, not that it's right.
- **Unchecked**: no Dolphin compare or golden covers it.

The golden files name no commit, so their rows give the gcn-recomp pin they're held to. `tools/nightly.py` plays the goldens and the triaged checkpoints on the real game each night. Its reports stay in `build/nightly/`, so none of its numbers are here.

Of 18 features, 6 are checked against Dolphin, 4 against our goldens only and 8 are unchecked. The Dolphin compare has 41 checkpoints on 3 routes, 30 `within`, 3 `timing`, 5 `unclear` and 3 `bug`.
